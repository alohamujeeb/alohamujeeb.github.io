## <font color='green'>Why AI Applications?</font>

AI becomes meaningful when it can be applied to real problems.

This section explores practical applications of AI across different domains, including examples, experiments, tools, and ideas that demonstrate what AI can actually do.

The focus is on **usefulness, experimentation, and learning from real applications**, rather than following trends for their own sake.
