# <font color='green'>Learning & Decision Methods</font>

*Explore how computational systems learn, adapt, search, and make decisions.*

---

## <font color='green'>Why Learning & Decision Methods?</font>

Intelligence is not achieved through a single method.

Different computational approaches provide different ways of learning from experience, finding solutions, adapting to changing conditions, and making decisions.

This section explores these approaches and how they can complement one another.

The emphasis is on **understanding the underlying ideas and seeing them work in practice**.
