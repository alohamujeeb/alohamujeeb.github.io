# Circular wisdom – A personal note


- Once upon a time, error handling in code was simple: just good old if-else checks. No drama, no flair.

- Then came try-catch, heralded as a cleaner, more elegant way to handle errors. And it quickly became the new standard in many languages.
<!-- more -->
- Fast forward a few decades, and the creators of Go took a step back and basically said, "Actually... that old if-else style? Kinda better. More explicit. Easier to follow."

	So they left try-catch out entirely.

- That said, for critical failures **Go does stick to try-catch-ish mechanism, just under a different name**: panic and recover.

- These "great" minds just love to introduces their own **wisdom** and masses just follow like crazy.

So... what the heck is going on? 😄 

(Still trying to make sense of all this circular wisdom.)
